About A2Z Placement Cell & Career Governance

Central Placement Nodal Office for Engineering, Computing & Applied Sciences

Official Policy & Guidelines →

The A2Z Placement Cell serves as the central bridge connecting graduating engineering talent with global technology companies, quantitative trading firms, and leading consultancies. Operating under strict institutional governance, verified academic credentials, and transparent interview scheduling.

DPC Verified Credentials
All 10th, 12th/Diploma percentages, semester SGPA, and master resumes are audited and certified by Placement Head.
Day 1 Superday Slots
Automated conflict-free slot allotment across Slot 1 (08:00 AM) and Slot 2 (02:00 PM) for parallel interviews.
1-Student-1-Job Policy
Institutional policy ensuring equal opportunity with Dream offer upgrade rules for candidates.
Corporate Enablement
Direct JAF submissions, custom academic cutoffs, candidate shortlisting, and live offer tracking for HRs.
