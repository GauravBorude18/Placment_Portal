Official Campus Placement Policy & Code of Conduct

Phase 1 & Phase 2 Recruitment Season Rules (Academic Year 2025-26)

Rule 1

One Job Policy & Dream Company Upgrade

Once a student secures an official campus placement offer, they are marked as Placed and deregistered from standard open drives. A student may apply for an upgrade only if the subsequent opening is designated as a Super Dream Category (Package ≥ 1.5x of current offer).

Rule 2

Verified Academic Credentials & DPC Sign-off

All 10th marks, 12th/Diploma percentages, subject breakdowns, semester SGPA pointers, and backlog status must be verified by the Department Placement Coordinator (DPC). Any falsification of data leads to immediate debarment.

Rule 3

Interview Punctuality & Anti-Ghosting Policy

Shortlisted candidates must appear for designated interview slots at least 15 minutes prior to scheduled time. Unexcused absence from an allocated slot results in a 14-day recruitment suspension.
