Campus Placement Policy & Student Guidelines

Governing regulations for students, corporate recruiters, and placement coordinators (A2Z Placement Cell)

RULE 1

One Job Policy & Dream Company Upgrade

Once a student secures an official campus placement offer, they are marked as Placed and deregistered from standard open drives. A student may apply for an upgrade only if the subsequent opening is designated as a Super Dream Category (Package ≥ 1.5x of current offer) or an International Placement Slot.

RULE 2

Verified Academic Credentials & DPC Sign-off

All 10th marks, 12th/Diploma percentages, subject breakdowns, semester SGPA pointers, and backlog status must be verified and signed off by the Department Placement Coordinator (DPC) and TPO Head. Any discrepancy or falsification of data leads to immediate cancellation of registration.

RULE 3

Interview Punctuality & Anti-Ghosting Policy

Shortlisted candidates must appear for designated interview slots at least 15 minutes prior to the scheduled time. Unexcused absence from an allocated slot or non-appearance results in a 14-day temporary recruitment suspension.

RULE 4

Professionalism, Fair Practice & Ethics

Students must maintain professional conduct during company interactions, online tests, and executive interview panels. Direct off-campus negotiation or bypassing the Placement Cell during active season is strictly prohibited.

🏢
Training & Placement Cell Support

For queries regarding dream category rules or recruitment slots, reach out to your coordinator.
